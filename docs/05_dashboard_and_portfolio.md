# Dashboard design and portfolio story

## Visual direction

“Boussole” means compass: the visual system uses deep teal for credibility, mint for positive momentum, amber for attention and warm off-white instead of generic dashboard gray. Manrope creates strong editorial headings; DM Sans keeps dense labels readable. Rounded cards, restrained shadows and generous whitespace make the project feel like a product, not a notebook.

The Sites design guidance influenced the first viewport: it starts with the product promise and evidence, not generic navigation chrome. Every page answers one decision-oriented question.

## Page narrative

1. **Market pulse** establishes size, geography, industries and recent listings.
2. **Skills radar** moves from individual skills to valuable combinations.
3. **People & pay** compares seniority, explicit languages and disclosed compensation.
4. **Demand trends** shows monthly market and skill trajectories.
5. **CV match** turns analysis into personal action.
6. **Data studio** makes provenance, coverage and refresh mechanics visible.

## How to present it in a portfolio

Lead with the problem: students and career-switchers see thousands of adverts but cannot observe aggregate demand. Then show the evidence chain—permitted source, raw listing, normalized record, extracted skills, warehouse query, chart. This demonstrates product thinking and data responsibility, not just visual polish.

A strong case study includes:

- a 30-second screen recording moving from market overview to a CV gap;
- a diagram of the four-layer architecture;
- one extraction error you found and fixed;
- a statement that the bundled data is synthetic;
- validation metrics from a labelled real sample;
- a before/after learning roadmap based on co-occurring skills;
- honest limitations around salary coverage and source representativeness.

## Accessibility and UX checks

Do not rely on color alone; labels and values remain visible. Keep chart titles as conclusions when publishing. Test keyboard navigation, narrow screens, French/Arabic text rendering and contrast. Plotly hover detail should supplement rather than replace axis labels.

## Suggested screenshots

Capture the market hero with KPIs, the co-occurrence chart, the salary coverage note, and one CV result using a fictional CV. Never place a real person’s CV or personal data in the repository or portfolio screenshots.
